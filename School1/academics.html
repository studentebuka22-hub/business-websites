Classes & Curriculum

A structured path from Creche through Primary 6, built for real learning at every stage.

Classes Offered

Every age, a clear next step

Creche

Ages 6 months – 2 years. Safe, nurturing care with early sensory and language stimulation.

Pre-Nursery & Nursery 1–2

Ages 2–5. Phonics, numeracy basics, motor skills, and guided play.

Primary 1–3

Ages 6–8. Core literacy and numeracy, science introduction, character education.

Primary 4–6

Ages 9–11. Full subject curriculum with common entrance exam preparation.

Extracurricular

Sports, music, art club, and debate — every child finds something they love.

Term Structure

Three terms per session, with mid-term breaks and continuous assessment.
